Our Story

Born of Driftwood, Flowers & Salt Air

Rustic Wonders is a one-of-a-kind handmade home goods business born from a passion for craftsmanship, creativity, and the timeless beauty of natural materials. Founded by artisan June Bauer on Long Island, New York, every creation is crafted entirely by hand — infused with care, character, and a deep respect for the materials that inspire each design.

From sun-bleached driftwood collected along Long Island's beaches to reclaimed barn wood and repurposed treasures given new life, each product is a unique work of art. No two pieces are ever exactly alike — that's not a limitation; it's the whole point.

Materials & Sustainability

Nature's Materials, Given New Life

Rustic Wonders is committed to natural, reclaimed, and repurposed materials wherever possible — giving each piece distinctive character while caring for the environment we draw inspiration from.

🌊 Driftwood

Gathered along Long Island's coastlines and beaches. Naturally weathered, smooth, and shaped by the sea — no two pieces alike.

🪵 Reclaimed Wood

Salvaged barn wood, old fencing, and furniture. Rich patina, nail holes, and knots that tell a story of a past life.

♻️ Repurposed & Recycled

Thrift finds, vintage pieces, salvaged metals, glass, and hardware — upcycled into entirely new art forms and functional décor.

🌲 Fresh-Cut & Natural Finishes

Locally sourced hardwoods with stains, oils, and beeswax. Non-toxic options prioritized; food-safe finishes available on request.
