meet the maker

Hi, I'm June! 👋

I wander Long Island's beaches collecting driftwood, forgotten treasures, and little sparks of inspiration — then I turn them into things that make people smile.

Rustic Wonders started with a simple belief: your home deserves more personality than a big-box store can deliver. Every birdhouse, sign, and keepsake I make carries a story — of salt air, of sunny mornings, of materials given a joyful second life. When you bring one home, that story becomes yours.

Where nature meets craftsmanship — every piece tells a story. 🌿
